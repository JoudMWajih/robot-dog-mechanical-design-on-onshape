# Robot Dog Mechanical Design

A simplified 3D mechanical design of a four-legged robot dog created using Onshape. The project includes the robot body, articulated legs, feet, joint pins, and a simplified servo motor.

## Components

* Robot Body
* Upper Leg
* Lower Leg
* Foot
* Joint Pin
* Servo Motor
* Complete Robot Dog Assembly

## Main Dimensions

* Robot body: 200 × 100 × 50 mm
* Upper leg: 50 × 20 × 6 mm
* Lower leg: 50 × 20 × 6 mm
* Foot: 35 × 25 × 8 mm
* Joint pin: Ø5 × 15 mm
* Simplified servo body: 23 × 12 × 29 mm

## STL Files

All individual components are provided as STL files for 3D printing.

## Software

* Onshape

## Project Purpose

This project demonstrates the mechanical design and assembly of a simplified robot dog with movable leg joints.
